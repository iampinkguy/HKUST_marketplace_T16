HKUST Marketplace is a JavaFX application for buying, selling, lending, and giving away items on campus. Accounts, listings, deals, reviews, and notifications are stored in data.db. Listing chat is stored in chat_history.json.

Passwords for new accounts must be at least 8 characters and include one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one digit. They are stored as BCrypt hashes with cost 12. See {@link com.hkust.marketplace.utils.DataService#checkPasswordStrength(String)}.
